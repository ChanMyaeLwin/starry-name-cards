# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome regardless of experience, identity, nationality, language, or ability. Be respectful, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Make space for learners and people using assistive technology.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, publishing another person's private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause discussions, warn participants, or restrict participation in response to harmful conduct. Responses should be proportionate, consistent, and protective of affected people. Maintainers are held to the same standards.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer using the private contact channel listed on their public profile. Do not post private details in an issue. A dedicated reporting contact must be configured by maintainers before public launch; no reporting address is invented here.
